How I think about campaigns

Don't start with the Ads Manager. Start with the business.

A campaign can be technically perfect and still fail if the offer, audience, creative or follow-up process is wrong. That's why METGEN follows a structured approach.

01 • DISCOVER

Business & Offer

Understand the service, pricing, market, location, competition and why someone should choose you.

02 • DEFINE

Ideal Customer

Map who is most likely to buy, what they care about and what problem they want solved.

03 • POSITION

Message & Creative

Create hooks, angles and creative concepts that communicate the offer quickly.

04 • BUILD

Campaign Architecture

Choose objectives, targeting, placements, budgets and tracking around the goal.

05 • TEST

Controlled Experiments

Compare meaningful variables and use the data to identify stronger combinations.

06 • OPTIMIZE

Improve & Scale

Reduce waste, improve the strongest elements and scale only when the economics support it.

My rule: Cheap clicks are not the goal. The goal is the right customer action at a cost that makes sense for the business.

FAQ

Common questions from potential clients.

What businesses do you work with?

I primarily focus on local and service-based businesses where paid advertising can create measurable inquiries, conversations or sales opportunities.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. Advertising is affected by the offer, market, competition, creative, targeting, budget, tracking and sales process. I focus on building and improving the system rather than making unrealistic guarantees.

Can you manage my existing Meta or Google Ads account?

Yes. I can review an existing setup and recommend whether to optimize, restructure or rebuild it.

Can customers contact you directly?

Yes. WhatsApp is the fastest way to start a conversation, and email is also available for business inquiries.

How much should I spend on ads?

There is no universal budget. The right testing budget depends on customer value, location, competition, objective and how much data the campaign needs to make useful decisions.
